# ACOR grounding architecture

ACOR is an approval boundary and operating method, not a second source of brand truth.

## Reuse

- Organisations provide tenant and market-operation ownership.
- Blotato destination accounts plus platform and objective provide learning isolation.
- MemBrain provides versioned, organisation-scoped authoritative knowledge and six-category readiness.
- Market Intelligence profiles, reviewed references and abstract patterns hold approved strategic context, provenance and hypotheses.
- Campaigns execute approved plans; attributed engagement snapshots and explicit commercial outcomes measure them separately.
- Existing audit/activity records show operational changes.

## Extend

- ACOR lifecycle, evidence approval, proof depth, deterministic Growth Readiness gates, honest baseline states and 30/60/90 metric families are generic domain rules.
- The Market Intelligence workspace presents Growth Readiness without converting the forensic report's 94/100 into a Genesis score.
- Cultural treatment may be selected per recommendation, but Light Naija is rejected unless approved organisation cultural context exists.

## New

- Local seed data grounds Client Zero through the same organisation-owned MemBrain and Market Intelligence records future clients use.
- No production client-name branching, new tables or market-entity concept is introduced.

## Approval flow

Evidence → proposed intelligence → human verification and approval → authoritative MemBrain or reviewed Market Intelligence.

Proposed, pending, unverifiable or rejected evidence cannot become authoritative knowledge. A reference in a client's possession is not automatically client proof, and inaccessible evidence remains pending rather than absent.

## Client Zero readiness

Villiz Pixels UK remains `GROUNDING`. Brand truth, MemBrain, Market Intelligence, conversion, reviewed priority-offer proof and Day-0 baseline can pass from local records. Platform connections and measurement remain explicit gaps until real connections and measurement exist.

## Strategic Growth Blueprint mapping

- Brand truth and primary audiences: authoritative MemBrain.
- Commercial North Star, market and conversion strategy: Market Intelligence profile.
- Battlefield, competitive position, discovery/search/content/visibility strategy, GBP plan and 30-day attack plan: reviewed abstract Market Intelligence patterns.
- Measurement: existing account-scoped performance snapshots and explicit commercial outcome records.
- Day-0 evidence and pending gates: reviewed Market Intelligence references.

This avoids duplicating client facts, metrics or outcomes in a blueprint subsystem.
